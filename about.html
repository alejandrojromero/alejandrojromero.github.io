About

Hey, I'm Alejandro!

I'm a designer and prototyper from Puerto Rico with a strong passion for making innovative experiences for all to enjoy.

My main foci are UX design, mixed reality, human-computer interaction, and mobile/web apps.

I took a game design course in high school that made me fall in love with tech and creating 3D experiences.

Outside of work, I do experimental art, motion graphics, and graphic design under the moniker aleboi. I also enjoy photography, cycling, hiking, gaming, drawing, and a whole lot more. Feel free to drop me a line 🔥
